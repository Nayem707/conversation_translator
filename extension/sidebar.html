    What can this extension hear?

      Audio is never stored. Microphone/tab recognition is performed by the browser’s speech service (Chrome sends audio to Google unless on-device recognition is used). Recognised text is sent only to the selected translation provider, and to the next provider only if that one fails. No analytics, accounts, or backend.
